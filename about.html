01Who we are

Very few companies bridge engineering design, manufacturing, material science and international sourcing under one roof. ZHUOJIA does.

We operate as a hybrid of engineering consultancy, advanced manufacturing firm and industrial product-development partner. This structure lets us manage projects from validation to prototype fabrication and full-scale production.

Clients work with one accountable team — not a chain of subcontractors — so specifications, quality and schedules stay aligned from the first drawing to the final inspection.
